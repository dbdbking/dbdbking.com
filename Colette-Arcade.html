Colette Arcade

In the perfect meeting of high fashion and technology, db-db has teamed up with iconic Parisian retailer Colette and 2015 LVMH Finalists JOURDEN, to create one of this season’s funniest and fashion-forward apps – a game where you actually take your clothes off!

To celebrate this launch, JOURDEN will be taking over the windows of Colette from April 27th – 3rd May and unveiling “Colette Arcade” where fashion lovers will be invited to download the “Colette Arcade” app and start shedding their designer duds!

Featuring key looks from JOURDEN’S SS15 collection in 8-bit pixels and soundtrack 'Colette C'est Chouette' by Busy P. Feat. Linlee Allen, fans can play, create and scan to choose outfits, style up their avatars and unlock more special looks via QR codes located in-store or online. Each JOURDEN outfit can also be purchased through Colette’s online store or in-store.
